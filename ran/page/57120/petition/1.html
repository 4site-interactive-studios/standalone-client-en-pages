Protect Critical Rainforests Now!

To avert climate catastrophe we must stop the destruction of the world's last remaining rainforests like Indonesia's Leuser Ecosystem. The Leuser Ecosystem holds massive amounts of carbon in its forests and peatlands, as well as one of the highest concentrations of biodiversity in the world.

Major snack brands are driving the destruction of critical rainforests, including the Leuser Ecosystem. Millions of acres of rainforest continue to be bulldozed to produce the cheap Conflict Palm Oil found in packaged foods.

Add your name to tell snack food companies to stop the destruction of critical forests like the Leuser Ecosystem.
