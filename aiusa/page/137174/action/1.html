TAKE ACTION FOR HUMAN RIGHTS

END ISRAEL'S GENOCIDE IN GAZA. DEMAND THAT JUSTICE IS SERVED

Despite a ceasefire agreement between Israel and Hamas, attacks by Israeli forces continue in the occupied Gaza Strip, Palestinian civilians are still being killed, and families are still facing mass starvation as the engineered famine continues.

We urgently need your help to continue pushing for a permanent halt to hostilities, an end to Israel’s genocide, and justice. We must raise our voices now to make sure our lawmakers know we won’t stand by while violations go unchecked.

Join us now: send your letter to your members of Congress and President Trump to urge them to:
1) ensure the ceasefire means a full end of hostilities
2) press Israel to allow full access to life-saving humanitarian aid and the entry of independent human rights monitors into Gaza
3) immediately suspend the sale and transfer of weapons to Israel, and
4) release all Palestinians who remain in arbitrary detention.
